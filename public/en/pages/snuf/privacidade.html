Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This policy explains how information may be processed while using the Snuf.

Information

Depending on the features used, information provided by Discord may be processed, such as user IDs, server IDs, settings, and messages sent to the bot.

Storage and processing

Information required for the service to work may be stored or processed through external services, including Firebase, Microsoft Azure/Microsoft Foundry, and Heroku.

Artificial intelligence features may send messages and contextual information to Microsoft Foundry for processing.

Privacy

We do not request passwords, tokens, or personal credentials for normal service operation, and we do not sell user information.

The amount and type of information processed may vary depending on the features used and as the service evolves.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service and its features evolve.

Questions or requests related to privacy may be sent through the project's official channels.
